About Me

Hello, I'm Chaunce Grant-Kusmider, an IMD student at Algonquin College. I'm driven by a passion for digital storytelling, and I specialize in music production, voice acting, and creative writing. Since June 22nd 2022, I have been producing original instrumentals, writing my own lyrics, recording my own tracks, and actively collaborating with other artists and producers.

Beyond sound design, I love geeking out over world-building and creative game concepts. In fact, I frequently write custom interpretations of popular video game genres, such as horror or hero shooters, featuring original characters created by me and my friends. I am always eager to connect, collaborate, and bring new multimedia ideas to real life.
