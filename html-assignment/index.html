About Me

Hello, I'm Chaunce Grant-Kusmider! I've recently become a student at Algonquin College, pursuing a degree in IMD. I'm passionate about producing music, voice acting, and creative writing, etc. With music production tools, I began to create instrumentals for myself to write and record on, as well as network with other producers or vocalists to either produce or record on a song starting from June 22nd, 2022. I also love to geek out by writing out my own versions of content that already exists in form of concepts. For example, I've written my own interpretations of popular game genres like horror or hero shooters using characters that me or my friends create.
